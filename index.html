About Us

Little Luxury is a made-to-order tailoring studio, cutting and finishing every piece by hand from carefully sourced fabric. Each garment carries the same attention a couture atelier gives its clients — fitted to you, not to a rack.

We work in small batches so quality never gets rushed, and every order is packed and checked personally before it leaves our studio.

Please note We currently accept prepaid orders only via secure online payment. We do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD) on any order.
